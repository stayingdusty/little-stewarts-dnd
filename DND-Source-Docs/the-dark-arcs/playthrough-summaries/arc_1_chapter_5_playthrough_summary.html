Chapter Snapshot

Chapter 5 begins the morning after the Academy of Sparks heist. The dwarven boss formally rewards the party for recovering the stolen Great Tree sap, confirms the Crucible District's support for Queen Flower, and presents each hero with a personalized gift or promise. A concealed security escort returns the party to the Ethereum Academy, where Sertraline discovers that Dr. Fizzlebig is secretly connected to the robed guard and is herself promoted to command a six-person unit. The party then pieces together the matching vaults from the river cruise and the Academy of Sparks, chooses an election instead of a trial, and splits its efforts between the people of the marketplace and the Academy of Antiquities.

Major Outcome

Queen Flower's inherited pendant is revealed to be only half of an ancient artifact. Beneath the Great Tree, Tindily Migrot reunites it with its missing half. The completed pendant projects a living map of every place it has previously traveled and hides untouched lands beneath fog. By tracing the map, Queen Flower gains limited astral projection and the ability to observe and interact with distant places connected to the pendant's history. Tindily allows her to keep the artifact and makes the Academy of Antiquities' council vote contingent on Queen Flower researching and reporting on its nature and use.

The Morning After the Academy of Sparks Heist

The chapter opens inside the Crucible District, where the party has been sheltered overnight following the successful recovery of the Great Tree sap. The dwarven boss wakes the crew and informs them that a security escort has arrived to take them back to the Ethereum Academy.

  • The party is no longer being treated as unknown outsiders. Their successful heist has earned them direct recognition from the Crucible District.
  • The dwarven boss fulfills the political portion of his agreement by providing Queen Flower with formal documentation of the district's council support.
  • He also gives or promises a personalized reward to each member of the party, choosing gifts that reflect their roles, abilities, and developing identities.
  • The morning establishes that the party's actions are now producing permanent political, financial, and magical consequences.

The Dwarven Rewards

The dwarven boss recognizes each hero separately rather than presenting the party with a single shared prize.

  • Golo receives a Dwarven Hammer. The weapon represents the dwarves' respect for his strength, his role in recovering the sap, and his growing connection to the Crucible District through the licensed Dwarven Fire Rune.
  • Sertraline is promised specialized bracers. The dwarven boss explains that the bracers are still being finished. When complete, they will form magical hand extensions and provide the equivalent of the Mage Hand cantrip, allowing her to manipulate distant objects.
  • Fiona receives a special admission ticket to the Feast of the Ancestors. The ticket gives her access to an important dwarven cultural and ceremonial event connected to lineage, memory, and the recovered Great Tree sap.
  • Queen Flower receives the Crucible District's council certification. This document serves as official proof that the district will cast its election vote in support of her claim.

Political payoff: The recovery of the Great Tree sap satisfies the condition for the Crucible District's council vote. Queen Flower now possesses documented support rather than only a verbal promise.

The Gold Reward

After returning to the Ethereum Academy, Dr. Fizzlebig distributes the financial reward provided by the dwarven boss.

  • Each of the four player characters receives 125 gold.
  • The total equals the 500 gold reward promised for returning the stolen Great Tree sap.
  • The equal division prevents the reward from belonging only to the character who physically recovered or carried the sap.
  • The payment makes the party substantially wealthier than they were during their early days at the Wayward Flame.
  • The chapter marks a major shift from surviving through hunting and rent arrangements to operating with meaningful financial resources.

The Smoke-Cloaked Escort

The security escort sent to retrieve the party is made up of robed figures surrounded by drifting smoky wisps. Their clothing and magic obscure their individual identities.

  • The guards do not travel openly as ordinary city watch officers.
  • The smoky wisps conceal their faces and make the entire entourage appear secretive and supernatural.
  • The escort guides the party safely from the Crucible District back toward the Ethereum Academy.
  • Their presence establishes that Veylathar contains covert security organizations operating alongside its visible institutions.

The Eyes on the Rooftops

While the party travels through Veylathar, Sertraline notices concealed eyes watching from the tops of nearby buildings.

  • Sertraline uses her existing telepathic ability to quietly alert the robed escort.
  • To her surprise, Dr. Fizzlebig answers her through thought.
  • Fizzlebig confirms that he also sees the rooftop observers.
  • He then reveals that he is connected to the guard and is not merely an academic administrator traveling with the party.
  • The exchange gives Sertraline privileged knowledge of Fizzlebig's covert role without requiring a public explanation in front of the entire group.

Expanded role: Dr. Fizzlebig is both an Ethereum Academy figure and a member or operative of the smoke-cloaked guard. His loyalties and responsibilities extend beyond scholarship.

Sertraline Becomes Commander of the Guard

Fizzlebig elevates Sertraline from ally of the guard to its commanding officer.

  • Sertraline receives her own smoky, wispy cloak, marking her as a recognized member and commander.
  • Her appointment is not merely ceremonial. She gains authority over a defined unit.
  • The unit contains six members total, including Sertraline.
  • The remaining five members are one alligator-person, two gnomes, one tiefling, and one human.
  • Fizzlebig is associated with the guard but is not counted as one of the six members in Sertraline's unit.
  • Sertraline can telepathically communicate with her companions through her existing powers.
  • Her new command status also allows her to sense the presence of her unit and recognize whether individual members are nearby.

The Guard's Levitation Staves

The smoke-cloaked guard carries enchanted staves designed for moving an entire protected group through dangerous areas.

  • The staves can levitate the full entourage rather than lifting only a single person.
  • The levitation allows the guard to pass over tripwires without touching them.
  • Pressure plates can be avoided because the group's weight never reaches the floor.
  • Magical floor runes can also be bypassed when their triggering condition requires physical contact.
  • The staves make the guard especially effective in trapped buildings, magical ruins, vaults, and hostile institutional environments.

Permanent guard mechanic: Sertraline's guard unit can use its staves to levitate an escorted group across floor-based traps, pressure plates, tripwires, and runes.

Return to the Ethereum Academy

The party reunites with Queen Flower at the Ethereum Academy. The return gives them a chance to compare what they have learned during the Academy of Sparks mission and earlier events.

  • The party discusses the vault located at the top of the Academy of Sparks.
  • They recognize that it is identical to the vault hidden aboard the river cruise boat from their arrival in Veylathar.
  • The repeated vault design is too specific to be treated as coincidence.
  • The party concludes that the mysterious man in the white suit is connected to both locations.
  • This links the opening river journey more directly to the current Academy conspiracy.
  • The discovery keeps the man in the white suit active as a major unresolved figure even though he does not directly appear in this chapter.

Trial or Election

The party considers two possible methods of removing the current Queen Regent and placing Queen Flower in power.

  • Formal trial: The party could attempt to expose wrongdoing, gather evidence, and legally depose the current queen through a public proceeding.
  • Council election: The party could avoid the risks of a trial and instead build a majority of district and academy votes for Queen Flower.
  • The trial path could force the party to prove accusations before the city's institutions and might reveal their enemies before they are ready.
  • The election path allows the party to focus on alliances, favors, public support, and legitimate council recognition.
  • The group chooses to pursue the election for Queen Flower and avoid initiating a formal trial against the current queen.

Campaign direction: The election becomes the party's official strategy. Council votes, political favors, and relationships with Veylathar's districts and academies now become primary objectives.

The Party Divides Its Political Work

Rather than pursuing only one institution at a time, the party separates to work on different sources of political influence.

  • Golo goes to the marketplace to speak with the Butcher and determine how support might be built among the market community.
  • His role focuses on relationships with common workers, tradespeople, and the people who operate Veylathar's daily economy.
  • Queen Flower, Fiona, Sertraline, and Dr. Fizzlebig travel toward the Academy of Antiquities.
  • Their goal is to meet the Academy's leader and secure another formal council vote.
  • The split creates two parallel forms of campaigning: Golo works through public trust while the others work through institutional recognition.

The Academy of Antiquities

The Academy of Antiquities is not a conventional tower. It is built as an enormous circular glass enclosure surrounding the Great Tree.

  • The public academy resembles a massive glass terrarium or botanical museum.
  • The Great Tree rises from the center of the structure and dominates the entire interior.
  • The circular outer wall is lined with display cases containing relics, magical objects, and historical artifacts.
  • The arrangement allows visitors to move around the edge of the dome while viewing both the museum collection and the Great Tree.
  • The upper level is open to scholars, visitors, and members of the public.
  • The most sensitive research and archival work takes place below the public museum level.

Location canon: The Academy of Antiquities is a giant circular glass dome built around the Great Tree, with museum-style artifact cases lining its perimeter.

June at the Information Desk

The party first approaches an information desk inside the public museum and meets an academy employee named June.

  • June initially resists the request to meet the head of the Academy.
  • Access to the Chief Archivist is not granted automatically, even to a claimant seeking a council vote.
  • Dr. Fizzlebig intervenes because he and June already know one another.
  • Fizzlebig charms and persuades her through their existing friendship rather than forcing access.
  • June retrieves a large key ring and personally guides the party to a restricted door at the edge of the glass dome.
  • The door opens into a descending route beneath the museum and beneath the Great Tree itself.

The Underground Archivist Cavern

The restricted level beneath the Academy is a massive subterranean chamber used by Tindily Migrot for research and archival study.

  • A staircase winds around the edge of the open cavern.
  • The ceiling is completely covered by the enormous living roots of the Great Tree.
  • The roots make the chamber feel as though the party has entered the hidden underside of the academy's central landmark.
  • Directly beneath the Great Tree's trunk hangs a circular portal resembling a dark, star-filled night sky.
  • The portal appears suspended at the center of the ceiling rather than resting on a wall or floor.
  • The exact destination, origin, and function of the starry portal are not explained during this chapter.
  • The cavern's inner study area is formed from a circular platform of granite.
  • A moat or barrier of liquid mercury separates the granite circle from the outer walkway.

The Mercury Bridge

The liquid mercury surrounding Tindily's study functions as both a barrier and a controlled doorway.

  • June selects a key from her key ring and places it into the corresponding lock.
  • Turning the key activates the mercury rather than opening a conventional hinged door.
  • The liquid metal rises or solidifies into a bridge across the moat.
  • The party crosses the temporary bridge onto the central granite platform.
  • The mechanism demonstrates that entry to the Chief Archivist's study requires both authorization and a specific academy key.

Security canon: Tindily Migrot's underground archive is protected by a mercury moat that forms a bridge only when activated with an authorized key.

Tindily Migrot, Chief Archivist

At the center of the cavern, the party meets Tindily Migrot, head of the Academy of Antiquities and its Chief Archivist.

  • Tindily has the head of a horse and the body of a human.
  • She wears spectacles suited to her scholarly work.
  • When the party arrives, she is reading a book in one hand while examining an artifact in the other.
  • Her posture and behavior establish her as a deeply focused researcher who studies written history and physical relics simultaneously.
  • She operates below the public museum rather than from a ceremonial office on the surface.
  • Her authority comes from her expertise as an archivist as well as her position as the Academy's leader.

Tindily Notices Queen Flower's Pendant

As the party steps onto the granite study platform, Tindily notices light emerging from Queen Flower's necklace.

  • The pendant had already been associated with Queen Flower's family and journey.
  • Tindily immediately recognizes that the artifact has deeper significance.
  • She asks Queen Flower where the pendant came from.
  • Queen Flower explains that it was handed down through her family, from her grandmother to her mother and eventually to her.
  • The answer connects the pendant to Queen Flower's inherited identity but does not fully explain its original creator or purpose.

The Missing Half Descends

Tindily reacts by summoning another artifact from the museum collection on the public level above.

  • She throws the artifact she had been studying upward toward the starry ceiling and roots.
  • Her action calls the second half of Queen Flower's pendant from one of the display cases around the glass dome.
  • The missing half descends through the space above the cavern and toward the Chief Archivist.
  • As it approaches, Queen Flower's inherited half begins to shake and vibrate violently.
  • The two objects recognize or respond to one another before physically touching.

Queen Flower Releases the Pendant

Queen Flower's response becomes as important to Tindily as the artifact itself.

  • Queen Flower does not clutch the pendant, demand ownership, or resist the possibility that it may be taken from her.
  • She calmly removes it from around her neck.
  • She releases it into the air and allows it to move toward its missing half.
  • The two pieces meet and unite in midair.
  • Tindily becomes intensely interested in Queen Flower's character because she gave up a source of inherited power without hesitation.
  • The moment demonstrates that Queen Flower does not measure leadership only through possession, control, or entitlement.

Character canon: Tindily's respect for Queen Flower begins when Queen Flower willingly releases the pendant. Her willingness to surrender power becomes evidence that she may be trustworthy enough to wield greater power.

The Unified Pendant

Once joined, the pendant becomes a complete artifact with a brighter and more powerful glow.

  • Tindily tells Queen Flower to keep the Unified Pendant rather than returning it to the museum collection.
  • The completed artifact's light is much stronger than the faint glow produced by the original half.
  • When Queen Flower puts it on, the pendant projects an illuminated map onto the cavern floor.
  • The map contains the places and routes connected to the pendant's previous physical travels.
  • Regions where the pendant has never traveled are obscured by fog.
  • The fog is not merely ordinary missing cartographic information. It is a magical boundary on the artifact's awareness and reach.

The Living Map of the Pendant's Journey

The projection records the pendant's history of movement rather than displaying the entire world automatically.

  • The map includes Peddletown, the true name of the party's original town.
  • It also includes the river route taken during the party's journey toward Veylathar.
  • Veylathar and other places physically visited by the pendant appear as known regions.
  • Unvisited areas remain covered in magical fog and cannot yet be explored through the pendant.
  • The map therefore grows as the pendant travels with Queen Flower and the party.
  • The artifact creates a mechanical reason for exploration: carrying it into a new region permanently expands the map and Queen Flower's possible projection range.

Exploration mechanic: The Unified Pendant only reveals locations it has physically visited. Traveling with the pendant removes fog from the map and creates new possible destinations for Queen Flower's astral projection.

Queen Flower Traces Peddletown

Queen Flower reaches toward the projection and touches the area representing Peddletown.

  • Her finger begins tracing the same route that the pendant and party previously traveled.
  • The vision does not begin as an intentional spell with known instructions.
  • Instead, Queen Flower discovers the power through direct interaction with the projected map.
  • The cavern fades from her immediate awareness as her perception moves into Peddletown.
  • She sees Golo's parents continuing their ordinary work and helping keep the town functioning.
  • The moment confirms that the vision is occurring in a distant real place rather than replaying only a symbolic memory.

The Visionary Flight

Queen Flower's consciousness follows the movement of her finger across the map.

  • She feels her hand tracing the route of the party's original journey.
  • Her awareness moves along that path as though she is flying over the world.
  • The movement rapidly accelerates until she is traveling at nearly light speed back toward Veylathar.
  • The experience connects the physical map, the route previously traveled by the pendant, and Queen Flower's distant perception.
  • Her astral presence is capable of reaching faraway places where the pendant has already been.
  • The power also allows a limited form of interaction with those distant locations, although the full limits of what she can touch, change, say, or influence are not yet completely defined.
  • The longer and farther Queen Flower follows the map, the more physically draining the projection becomes.

Collapse from Exhaustion

The projection ends when Queen Flower can no longer sustain it.

  • Her consciousness races back toward the Academy of Antiquities.
  • She awakens or returns to herself on the floor of the granite cavern.
  • The other characters are gathered around and watching her recover.
  • The collapse establishes that the pendant's power has an immediate physical cost.
  • The artifact is not unlimited teleportation and does not allow effortless observation of every known place.
  • Distance, duration, focus, and degree of interaction may all contribute to the exhaustion caused by use.

Power limitation: Queen Flower's astral projection is limited and exhausting. Extended tracing or distant projection can cause her to collapse.

Tindily's Research Request

Tindily explains that she has long wondered about the history and purpose of the two pendant halves.

  • The Academy possessed one half but did not fully understand the complete artifact.
  • Queen Flower's family preserved the other half across multiple generations.
  • Reuniting them reveals functions that Tindily could not study while the pendant remained divided.
  • Tindily asks Queen Flower to keep the Unified Pendant for a period of practical research.
  • Queen Flower is expected to experiment carefully, learn what the pendant can do, and report her findings to the Academy.
  • The request converts the pendant from a simple inherited keepsake into an active research quest.

The Academy of Antiquities Council Vote

Tindily does not immediately guarantee the Academy's political support.

  • She tells Queen Flower that she will consider granting the Academy of Antiquities' council vote.
  • Her decision will depend on the results of Queen Flower's research and how she handles the pendant's power.
  • Tindily's test is not solely academic. It is also a test of judgment, honesty, restraint, and leadership.
  • Queen Flower must return with useful information rather than simply keeping the artifact as a personal advantage.
  • The Academy's vote becomes tied to a concrete quest rather than a speech or persuasion check alone.

Items and Artifacts

  • Dwarven Hammer: A dwarven-made weapon granted to Golo as recognition for his role in the recovery of the Great Tree sap.
  • Mage Hand Bracers: Custom bracers being completed for Sertraline. They form magical hand extensions and are intended to grant Mage Hand-like manipulation at a distance.
  • Feast of the Ancestors Ticket: A special admission item allowing Fiona to attend an important dwarven ceremony.
  • Crucible District Vote Certification: Formal proof that the Crucible District will cast its council vote for Queen Flower.
  • Wispy Command Cloak: A smoke-shrouded robe identifying Sertraline as Commander of the covert guard unit.
  • Guard Levitation Staves: Enchanted staves capable of levitating an entire entourage over floor-based traps and runes.
  • Unified Pendant: The completed form of Queen Flower's inherited pendant, able to project a living map and support limited astral projection to previously visited places.
  • Academy Artifact Keys: June's key ring contains keys that grant access to restricted areas, including the mechanism that creates the mercury bridge.

New NPCs and Expanded Roles

  • June: Information-desk worker and archivist at the Academy of Antiquities. She initially protects access to Tindily but cooperates because of her friendship with Dr. Fizzlebig.
  • Tindily Migrot: Horse-headed Chief Archivist and leader of the Academy of Antiquities. She studies beneath the Great Tree and controls the Academy's council vote.
  • Dr. Fizzlebig: Revealed to have a covert role within the smoke-cloaked guard in addition to his position at the Ethereum Academy.
  • The Butcher: Continues as Golo's connection to the marketplace and becomes a potential organizer or influencer in Queen Flower's election campaign.
  • Sertraline's Guard Unit: A six-member covert team consisting of Sertraline, an alligator-person, two gnomes, a tiefling, and a human.

Character Development

  • Queen Flower: Moves from symbolic claimant to active political candidate. Her willingness to release the pendant demonstrates humility, while the Unified Pendant gives her a powerful but exhausting way to remain connected to faraway people and places.
  • Sertraline: Gains formal command, a covert unit, a magical uniform, and a new responsibility beyond the immediate adventuring party. Her telepathy now supports both friendship and organized leadership.
  • Golo: Begins working independently as a political organizer. Instead of relying on strength or combat, he uses his relationship with the Butcher and the marketplace community to build support.
  • Fiona: Receives access to the Feast of the Ancestors, creating a future opportunity for her to participate in dwarven culture, spiritual tradition, and the consequences of recovering the Great Tree sap.
  • Dr. Fizzlebig: Becomes more complex as a mentor and ally. His secret guard affiliation reveals that he has been operating within hidden political and security structures.

Chapter Transition

Chapter 5 closes with the party transformed from successful adventurers into active political actors. The Crucible District has paid its reward and formally committed its council vote. Sertraline now commands a covert magical guard unit, Golo has begun organizing support in the marketplace, and Fiona has been invited into the dwarves' most important ancestral ceremony. Most significantly, Queen Flower now carries the completed pendant of her family line. Its living map connects her to the places it has traveled, while the fog surrounding unknown lands creates a direct link between exploration and power. The pendant gives Queen Flower a way to observe and interact with distant places, but her first use leaves her collapsed from exhaustion. Tindily Migrot has not yet promised the Academy of Antiquities' vote; Queen Flower must first prove that she can study, understand, and responsibly use the artifact. The next chapter can follow the party's widening election campaign, the pendant research, Golo's work with the Butcher, Sertraline's new command, or Fiona's invitation to the Feast of the Ancestors.
