Chapter Snapshot

Chapter 6 culminates in an arena battle fought on a platform suspended high above Veylathar. As the contest ends, the man in the white suit—known publicly in the city as Jerry Mander—uses snap matches to teleport from the spectator stands to the arena platform and disables the charms keeping it aloft. The entire arena begins falling toward the city. Jerry then attempts to reach and capture Queen Flower, but she surprises him, steals the snap matches from his hand, and snaps down to the falling platform. Fiona calls upon Aureth for guidance and, after one match proves insufficient, uses roughly one hundred snap matches together to pull the arena and everyone within the resulting dark arc blip into the Ethereal Realm. Aureth then returns the survivors to the Wayward Flame.

Major Outcome

Veylathar is saved from catastrophic destruction, but Jerry Mander escapes with his cronies using a separate supply of snap matches. The party convenes the city council and begins a formal investigation. Garoth reports that Jerry sometimes enters the Material Realm first as a rounded, indistinct shape before taking the form of a man. Dr. Fizzlebig explains that dark arcs are tears in reality: temporary blips may heal, but repeated damage can make them permanent. Every known dark arc bends toward the same central location. The party ends the chapter seeking Jerry, questioning his true nature, and investigating whether the wounds in reality can be healed.

The Arena Suspended Above Veylathar

The chapter's central crisis begins at the conclusion of an arena battle held on a magically suspended platform above Veylathar.

  • The arena is not located at ground level. It floats directly over the city through a network of magical suspension charms.
  • The party and other combatants are on the arena platform, while spectators watch from designated seating and viewing boxes.
  • Queen Flower watches from the Queen's box, placing her away from the main arena platform when the sabotage begins.
  • The location creates an enormous public spectacle but also places the city itself beneath the battlefield.
  • Once the suspension magic fails, the arena becomes a falling weapon capable of crushing buildings, streets, and citizens below.

Location canon: The Chapter 6 arena battle takes place on a magically suspended arena platform positioned in the sky over Veylathar.

Jerry Mander Leaves the Spectator Stands

The man in the white suit is present among the spectators when he begins his attack.

  • His commonly used name in Veylathar is Jerry Mander.
  • He carries snap matches in a matchbox and activates them by striking them along the side of the box.
  • A snap match allows its user to pass through the Ethereal Realm and reappear elsewhere.
  • The ability resembles a magical misstep or short-range teleport, but its movement specifically travels through the Ethereal Realm rather than simply crossing physical space.
  • Jerry strikes a match and disappears from his spectator seat.
  • He reappears directly on the arena platform where the battle has been conducted.

The Suspension Charms Are Disabled

Jerry's first objective is not to attack the party directly. He sabotages the magic keeping the arena suspended.

  • Jerry reaches the mechanisms or charms responsible for holding the arena in the air.
  • He disables the suspension enchantments.
  • The arena begins descending uncontrollably toward the city.
  • The falling platform threatens everyone on the arena and everyone living beneath it.
  • The crisis immediately changes the party's goal from winning an arena battle to preventing mass destruction.
  • Jerry's plan demonstrates that he is willing to endanger the entire city in order to achieve his larger purpose.

Threat escalation: Jerry Mander is no longer only a hidden manipulator associated with matching vaults and covert activity. He openly sabotages a public event and places Veylathar at risk of catastrophic destruction.

The Attempted Capture of Queen Flower

After disabling the arena's support magic, Jerry uses another snap match to reach the Queen's viewing area.

  • Jerry snaps away from the falling platform and reappears in the Queen's box.
  • His apparent goal is to steal, abduct, or capture Queen Flower during the confusion.
  • He expects to arrive, seize the Queen, and use the snap matches to escape before she can resist.
  • The attempted abduction confirms that Queen Flower is personally important to Jerry's plans.
  • The attack also shows that the collapsing arena may have served as both a citywide threat and a diversion for taking the Queen.

Queen Flower Takes the Snap Matches

Queen Flower surprises Jerry before he can secure the matches or complete the abduction.

  • She reacts quickly enough to snatch the snap matches away from Jerry.
  • Rather than remaining in the comparative safety of the viewing box, she strikes a match herself.
  • Queen Flower snaps down to the falling arena platform to rejoin the others and help address the danger.
  • Her decision prevents Jerry from using that specific matchbox to take her away.
  • The action places her in immediate danger but gives the party access to the same Ethereal travel mechanism Jerry has been exploiting.

Character moment: Queen Flower turns an attempted kidnapping into an act of resistance. She takes Jerry's escape tool and uses it to move toward the danger threatening her city rather than away from it.

Fiona Calls to Aureth

With the arena still falling, Fiona calls upon Aureth, her deity and divine source as a paladin.

  • Fiona asks for divine help while the party has only moments to prevent the arena from striking Veylathar.
  • Aureth answers her call directly.
  • Rather than removing the arena immediately, Aureth recommends using the snap matches to move the descending platform into the Ethereal Realm.
  • The proposed solution uses Jerry's own teleportation method against the disaster he created.
  • Aureth's guidance implies that snap matches are capable of moving more than a single traveler when enough of their power is combined.
Aureth's answer directs Fiona to use the snap matches to bring the falling arena into the Ethereal Realm, where Aureth can receive it and prevent it from destroying the city.

The First Attempt Fails

Fiona first attempts the plan with a single snap match.

  • One match is sufficient for the ordinary teleportation of a person through the Ethereal Realm.
  • It is not powerful enough to move an entire arena platform and everything upon it.
  • The first attempt fails to remove the descending structure.
  • Aureth answers again and tells Fiona that she will need more than one match.
  • The failed attempt establishes that the scale of a snap is tied to the number or combined magical force of the matches used.

Approximately One Hundred Snap Matches

Fiona escalates the attempt dramatically by activating a large portion of the available supply at once.

  • The matchbox or collected supply contains approximately 150 snap matches before the large-scale attempt.
  • Fiona uses roughly 100 snap matches simultaneously.
  • The exact number is approximate because the action occurred rapidly during the emergency.
  • The combined activation produces an effect far larger than the normal single-person snap.
  • Instead of creating a small point of teleportation, the matches generate a large dark arc blip around the arena.
  • Approximately fifty matches may remain from that supply, subject to later inventory confirmation.

The Dark Arc Blip Forms

The mass activation of snap matches produces a dark, curved rupture around the falling arena.

  • The effect appears as a large dark arc or blip in reality.
  • The arc encompasses the arena platform and the people within its affected space.
  • The arena does not merely stop falling. It is removed from the Material Realm.
  • Everything inside the dark arc is transported into the Ethereal Realm.
  • The falling momentum no longer threatens Veylathar because the arena is no longer physically present above the city.
  • The event saves the city but also becomes one of the largest known deliberate stresses placed upon the boundary between realms.

Major magical event: Fiona's simultaneous use of roughly one hundred snap matches creates a dark arc blip large enough to transport the entire falling arena into the Ethereal Realm.

Aureth Receives the Arena in the Ethereal Realm

Once the arena reaches the Ethereal Realm, Aureth is able to bring the crisis to an end.

  • Aureth's plan succeeds because the arena is shifted into the realm where Aureth is present and able to intervene.
  • Veylathar is no longer in the arena's path.
  • The people on the arena survive the transfer rather than being left to fall onto the city.
  • The event confirms that Aureth can directly assist Fiona when circumstances, faith, and access to the Ethereal Realm align.
  • Aureth's intervention does not erase the magical consequences of the mass snap, but it prevents immediate destruction.

Return to the Wayward Flame

After the arena has been removed from danger, Aureth transports the party and the other survivors back to the Wayward Flame.

  • The Wayward Flame remains the party's residence and operational home base in Veylathar.
  • Aureth returns everyone there once the city is safe and the main emergency has ended.
  • The transition brings the party from a massive public catastrophe back to a familiar location associated with safety, rest, and community.
  • The Wayward Flame becomes the place from which the party begins processing the event and organizing its response.
  • Kara Emberfall and Anja Willowbark remain the established proprietor and cook-bartender of the party's home, although no new direct actions by them were established in this playthrough account.

Jerry Mander and His Cronies Escape

Queen Flower's resistance prevents Jerry from capturing her, but it does not result in his arrest.

  • Jerry possesses or reaches a separate set of snap matches after Queen Flower takes the first set from him.
  • He uses the alternate matches to escape.
  • His cronies disappear with him.
  • Their destination is not known at the end of the chapter.
  • The escape confirms that Jerry has access to multiple supplies of snap matches and may have a broader network capable of producing, storing, or distributing them.
  • His ability to flee through the Ethereal Realm makes ordinary tracking methods less effective.

Unresolved enemy status: Jerry Mander survives the arena crisis and escapes with his cronies. He remains at large and retains access to additional snap matches.

The Party Begins an Investigation

After escaping the immediate disaster, the party turns from rescue to investigation.

  • The heroes begin gathering everything known about Jerry Mander.
  • They treat the arena sabotage and attempted kidnapping as connected parts of a larger plan.
  • The investigation builds upon earlier suspicions created by the identical vaults found on the river cruise and at the Academy of Sparks.
  • Jerry's routine use of the Ethereal Realm becomes a central line of inquiry.
  • The party seeks information not only about where he went, but about what kind of being he may actually be.

The Emergency City Council Meeting

The party calls a meeting with Veylathar's city council leadership and questions the city's major figures about Jerry.

  • The meeting gives the investigation an official civic and political dimension.
  • Queen Flower and the party question council members and leaders about their past observations of Jerry.
  • The investigation is no longer restricted to the party's private suspicions.
  • Jerry's attack on the city and attempted seizure of Queen Flower justify coordinated action by Veylathar's institutions.
  • The meeting also demonstrates Queen Flower functioning as an active ruler or civic leader rather than only an election claimant.

Garoth Joins the City Council

Garoth, previously encountered as the boss fight during the Academy of Sparks chapter, is now part of the city council.

  • His role has changed from direct antagonist or institutional defender to participating member of Veylathar's leadership.
  • The party is able to question him in his new council capacity.
  • His history within the Academy of Sparks gives him useful experience with unusual magical events and security threats.
  • His testimony becomes one of the most important new clues about Jerry's physical nature.

Garoth's Observation of Jerry's Arrival

Garoth reports that Jerry does not always appear immediately in the full shape of a human man when entering from the Ethereal Realm.

  • Garoth has observed Jerry teleporting from the Ethereal Realm on more than one occasion.
  • During some arrivals, Jerry first appears as a rounded, circular, or indistinct shape.
  • The shape changes as it enters the normal realm.
  • It becomes the recognizable form of a man only after the transition is underway or complete.
  • The observation raises the possibility that Jerry's humanoid appearance is adopted rather than natural.
  • The party does not yet know whether he is a shapeshifter, an Ethereal creature, a disguised entity, or something else entirely.

Queen Flower Gives Garoth a Rocky-Talky

Queen Flower establishes a direct communication channel with Garoth so that the investigation can continue outside formal meetings.

  • She gives Garoth a rocky-talky.
  • The device allows Garoth and the party to communicate across distance.
  • Garoth can report new information, sightings, or developments without waiting for another council session.
  • The decision turns Garoth into an active investigative contact rather than a one-time witness.
  • The rocky-talky network becomes part of the party's growing ability to coordinate allies throughout Veylathar.

Dr. Fizzlebig Understands the Dark Arcs

The party reports the mass snap event and its observations to Dr. Fizzlebig.

  • Fizzlebig connects the newly observed dark arc blip to the larger pattern of dark arcs seen in other places.
  • He explains that the arcs are not ordinary portals or harmless visual effects.
  • They are rips, tears, or holes in reality created by stress upon the boundary between realms.
  • A fresh tear may begin as a temporary dark arc blip.
  • Reality can sometimes repair or heal a new blip on its own.
  • Repeated tearing at the same place, or sufficient accumulated damage, can make the wound unhealable through normal natural repair.
  • Once the tear can no longer close, it becomes a permanent dark arc.

Dark Arc definition: A dark arc is a lasting wound in reality. Temporary dark arc blips may heal, but repeated or severe tearing can leave a permanent opening or scar.

Snap Matches and Damage to Reality

Fizzlebig's explanation reframes snap matches as dangerous tools whose convenience carries a hidden cost.

  • Each snap match opens or exploits a path through the Ethereal Realm.
  • Ordinary use may create only a small, temporary disruption that reality can repair.
  • Frequent travel, repeated use in the same locations, or unusually large activations may deepen the damage.
  • Jerry's regular dependence on snap matches may be contributing deliberately or carelessly to the formation of dark arcs.
  • Fiona's emergency use of roughly one hundred matches was necessary to save Veylathar but also created a major tear.
  • The party must now consider both the usefulness and the long-term danger of using the remaining matches.

All Dark Arcs Converge on One Place

Dr. Fizzlebig reveals that the dark arcs found in different cities and locations are connected by their direction and shape.

  • The arcs are geographically separated and appear in multiple places.
  • They do not curve randomly.
  • Every known dark arc bends or points toward the same central location.
  • The exact identity of that location is not established during this chapter.
  • The shared destination suggests a single source, anchor, destination, or force connecting the tears.
  • The convergence transforms the dark arcs from isolated magical accidents into evidence of a larger world-scale pattern.

Arc-defining revelation: The different dark arcs all converge toward one central point. Finding and understanding that point becomes a major objective of Arc 1.

The Question of Healing Reality

The party responds to Fizzlebig's explanation by asking whether dark arcs can be repaired.

  • The heroes are no longer satisfied with preventing new disasters one at a time.
  • They want to determine whether existing permanent dark arcs can be healed or closed.
  • The difference between a temporary blip and a permanent arc suggests there may be a stage at which intervention is still possible.
  • No confirmed healing method is established during this playthrough.
  • Research may require Dr. Fizzlebig, other academies, divine assistance, Ethereal exploration, or investigation of the convergence point.
  • The possibility of healing the arcs becomes both a scientific question and a central heroic purpose.

Items and Artifacts

  • Snap Matches: Enchanted matches struck against a matchbox to pass through the Ethereal Realm and reappear elsewhere. Individual matches support ordinary personal movement, while mass activation can move enormous structures and create dangerous dark arc blips.
  • Jerry's Seized Match Supply: The collection taken from Jerry during the attempted abduction. It contained approximately 150 matches before Fiona used roughly 100 during the arena rescue. The remaining count should be confirmed in future play.
  • Jerry's Alternate Snap Matches: A separate set used by Jerry and his cronies to escape after Queen Flower took the first supply.
  • Rocky-Talky Given to Garoth: A distance-communication device allowing Garoth to coordinate directly with Queen Flower and the party during the continuing investigation.
  • Arena Suspension Charms: The enchantments that originally held the arena platform above Veylathar. Jerry successfully disabled them.

NPCs and Expanded Roles

  • Jerry Mander: The public or commonly used name of the man in the white suit. He sabotages the arena, attempts to capture Queen Flower, escapes with cronies, travels frequently through the Ethereal Realm, and may conceal a non-human form.
  • Aureth: Fiona's paladin deity. Aureth answers Fiona during the falling-arena crisis, advises using multiple snap matches, receives the arena in the Ethereal Realm, and returns the survivors to the Wayward Flame.
  • Garoth: Former Academy of Sparks boss encounter and current city council member. He becomes a key witness and continuing investigative ally after describing Jerry's unusual arrival form.
  • Dr. Fizzlebig: Interprets the arena's dark arc blip and explains the progressive damage that creates permanent dark arcs. He also reveals that all known arcs converge toward one central point.
  • Jerry's Cronies: Unnamed accomplices who escape with Jerry using snap matches. Their identities, number, and functions remain unestablished.

Chapter Transition

Chapter 6 closes after the party's greatest public act of heroism so far. The arena that should have destroyed Veylathar is removed from the sky through Fiona's faith, Aureth's guidance, Queen Flower's defiance, and the overwhelming combined power of the stolen snap matches. The city survives, but the rescue exposes the hidden danger behind the matches: every passage through the Ethereal Realm can stress reality, and sufficient damage can leave a permanent dark arc. Jerry Mander escapes, but he can no longer remain merely the mysterious man associated with matching vaults. He has sabotaged a public arena, endangered an entire city, attempted to seize Queen Flower, and revealed that he has access to a larger supply network and a group of accomplices. Garoth's testimony raises an even deeper question about whether Jerry is truly human. Dr. Fizzlebig's discovery gives the arc its central direction: the wounds scattered across different places all bend toward the same point. The party now has two connected missions—find Jerry Mander and discover whether the dark arcs he exploits can be healed before reality is damaged beyond repair.
