Character Overview

Calvin is bonded to an ancient and powerful Dog Spirit. In moments of danger, loyalty, or protection, he can unleash the spirit as Nameloc—a massive glowing guardian hound.

The spirit chose him for his courage, instinct, and willingness to stand between danger and his friends.

Spirit Form, Attacks, Inventory & Session Tracking

Unleash the Great Hound

Once per session, Calvin releases the full Dog Spirit as a 10–15-foot glowing guardian hound.

  • Transform: Calvin becomes Nameloc.
  • Summon: Nameloc appears beside Calvin as a separate guardian presence.

Limitation: After Spirit Form ends, Calvin becomes tired and cannot use it again until the next session.
