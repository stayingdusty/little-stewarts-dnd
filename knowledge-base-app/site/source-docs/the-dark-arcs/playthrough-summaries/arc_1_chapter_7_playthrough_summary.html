Chapter Snapshot

Chapter 7 takes place the day after the emergency city council meeting. With Jerry Mander missing and Veylathar still unsettled, the heroes do not travel as a single party. Each follows a personal responsibility: Fiona inspects the city's military, Golo returns to the Crucible, Sertraline tests her connection to nature and investigates the Luminary Dome, and Queen Flower begins working through the responsibilities of rulership.

Major Outcome

The chapter establishes four new campaign threads. Fiona discovers serious neglect and possible theft within the southern garrison. Golo learns that Earthshatter now carries a heartbeat. Sertraline discovers a lens beneath the Luminary Dome aimed toward an Ethereal platform. Queen Flower is shown a full map of the wider world, opening the campaign beyond Veylathar.

The Day After the Council

The previous chapter ended with Veylathar saved from the falling arena, Jerry Mander escaped, and the city's leaders gathered to investigate the damage between the Material and Ethereal Realms. Chapter 7 begins quietly by comparison.

  • The heroes remain in Veylathar rather than immediately pursuing Jerry.
  • They separate during the day and begin acting through the positions, relationships, and authority they have gained.
  • The chapter focuses on the condition of the city, the heroes' individual responsibilities, and mysteries that may shape the next expedition.
  • No single shared combat encounter defines the session.

Chapter focus: Chapter 7 is a leadership and discovery chapter. Each hero learns something about the city, the world, or the power now connected to them.

Fiona — High General of Veylathar

Fiona begins her day simply, leaving the Wayward Flame in search of a turkey for a meal. Her new military authority becomes apparent almost immediately.

  • Guards and Sertraline's Wisp Cloaks observe Fiona's movement through the city.
  • A captain from the garrison personally ensures that Fiona's request is fulfilled.
  • The attention surrounding a simple errand shows that Fiona's new title already changes how the city's military responds to her.

Inspection of the Southern Garrison

Fiona later tours the southern garrison and finds that the institution she now helps lead has been badly neglected.

  • The soldiers are poorly fed.
  • Their uniforms are tattered and inadequately maintained.
  • The garrison roof leaks.
  • The treasury is not properly secured and remains vulnerable to thieves.
  • The combined problems suggest long-term underfunding, weak oversight, and internal decay rather than one isolated maintenance issue.

Fiona's Response

  • Fiona commits herself to pursuing increased funding for the garrison.
  • She begins considering broader military reforms rather than accepting the garrison's condition as normal.
  • She requests the use of Sertraline's Wisp Cloaks and royal guards to protect the treasury until stronger repairs and security can be established.
  • The possibility that money has already been stolen creates a new investigation: the garrison theft mystery.

Character milestone: Fiona's first major challenge as High General is not an enemy army. It is a neglected institution whose soldiers, buildings, and treasury all require attention.

Golo — The Crucible and Earthshatter

Despite his new role as a chief advisor, Golo returns to work in the Crucible District.

  • His return surprises his dwarven foreman.
  • Golo continues working at the forge rather than separating himself from the labor and community that helped establish him in Veylathar.
  • While he works, he senses an unfamiliar rhythm within his dwarven hammer, Earthshatter.

The Hammer's Heartbeat

  • Earthshatter pulses with a slow, steady heartbeat.
  • Each strike against iron causes the heartbeat to quicken.
  • The pulse also grows stronger as Golo continues working.
  • The hammer appears to respond to effort, stone, and steel.
  • Golo does not yet understand whether the hammer is awakening naturally or responding to an outside force.

Artifact development: Earthshatter no longer feels like an ordinary enchanted weapon. It now feels alive.

Sertraline — Nature and Knowledge

Sertraline enters the woods to test her growing connection to nature and to seek understanding of the dark arcs.

Communion with the Tree

  • Sertraline attempts to communicate with a tree about the dark arcs.
  • She does not receive a spoken explanation or a reasoned answer.
  • Instead, the tree communicates through raw sensory impressions.
  • The impressions include earth, decay, and life.
  • The experience establishes that nature can speak to Sertraline, but it does not reason or explain itself in the same way a person would.
Nature offers Sertraline experience and sensation rather than a direct answer.

Investigation at the Luminary Dome

After the communion, Sertraline turns from nature toward investigation.

  • She gathers Garoth and seeks Dr. Fizzlebig at the Luminary Dome.
  • Beneath the great tree, they observe a mysterious lens.
  • The lens is aimed toward an Ethereal platform.
  • Its alignment suggests that some mechanism beneath the dome is still watching or studying the other realm.
  • The builder, controller, and exact purpose of the lens remain unknown at the end of the chapter.

New mystery: The Luminary Dome contains a device positioned to observe an Ethereal platform, implying organized study or surveillance across the boundary between realms.

Queen Flower — The Map of the World

Queen Flower remains in the throne room and spends the day working through questions of governance and responsibility.

  • Her focus shifts from campaigning for authority to deciding how that authority should be used.
  • She considers the needs of Veylathar while the city continues recovering from Jerry Mander's attack.
  • During this work, a chalkboard turns and reveals a full map of the world.

The Wider World Revealed

  • The map contains cities, rivers, deserts, and distant lands.
  • Queen Flower sees clearly that Veylathar is only one part of a much larger world.
  • The problems surrounding Jerry Mander and the dark arcs may extend far beyond the city.
  • The map creates the possibility of choosing future regions for investigation, diplomacy, or expedition.

Campaign transition: The world map marks the point where the campaign begins opening beyond Veylathar. The heroes now have both a home to protect and a wider world to explore.

Items, Artifacts, and Locations

  • Earthshatter: Golo's dwarven hammer. During work at the Crucible, it begins pulsing with a heartbeat that strengthens and quickens with each strike.
  • Wisp Cloaks: Sertraline's cloaked agents or guards continue operating within Veylathar. Fiona requests their help securing the garrison treasury.
  • The Southern Garrison: A military post suffering from poor food, damaged uniforms, a leaking roof, and inadequate treasury security.
  • The Luminary Dome Lens: A mysterious device beneath the great tree, aligned toward an Ethereal platform.
  • The World Map: A full map revealed in the throne room, showing cities, rivers, deserts, and distant lands beyond Veylathar.

NPCs and Expanded Roles

  • The Garrison Captain: Personally responds to Fiona's request and helps demonstrate the practical authority attached to her new title.
  • Golo's Dwarven Foreman: Is surprised that Golo returns to ordinary work at the Crucible despite becoming a chief advisor.
  • Garoth: Continues serving as an investigative ally and accompanies Sertraline during the inquiry at the Luminary Dome.
  • Dr. Fizzlebig: Is sought at the Luminary Dome as Sertraline investigates the Ethereal platform and the mysterious lens.
  • Judith: Remains a possible source of information concerning Tindily Migrot's absence and secrecy.
  • Tindily Migrot: Her absence and secrecy remain unresolved and may be connected to activity beneath the Luminary Dome.

Character Development

  • Fiona: Begins acting as an institutional leader. She recognizes that protecting Veylathar requires budgets, repairs, logistics, security, and reform in addition to battlefield strength.
  • Golo: Remains connected to the dwarves and the physical work of the Crucible even after gaining political influence. His bond with Earthshatter deepens as the weapon begins displaying signs of life.
  • Sertraline: Learns that her connection to nature offers impressions rather than simple answers. She also continues developing as an investigator of the Ethereal Realm.
  • Queen Flower: Moves from seeking rulership to carrying its responsibilities. The world map forces her to consider Veylathar within a much larger political and geographic world.
  • The Party as a Whole: Functions independently without losing a shared direction. Each hero uncovers a different part of the next stage of the campaign.

Active Quest Threads

  • Jerry Mander: Determine where the man in the white suit fled after the council investigation began.
  • Garrison Theft Mystery: Identify who has been robbing or exploiting the southern garrison treasury and why.
  • Military Reform: Secure funding, improve food and uniforms, repair the leaking garrison, and establish dependable oversight.
  • Earthshatter's Awakening: Learn whether living tools are known among the dwarves and whether the hammer is awakening on its own.
  • The Luminary Dome Lens: Determine who built it, who controls it, and why it is aimed at the Ethereal platform.
  • Tindily Migrot's Absence: Question Judith and investigate whether Tindily's secrecy is connected to the Luminary Dome.
  • The Wider World: Decide which region, city, or dark-arc location should receive the party's attention first.

Campaign State at the End of Chapter 7

Veylathar: Safe from the falling arena but still recovering, politically unsettled, and facing internal neglect within its military.

Jerry Mander: Still missing after escaping Chapter 6.

Fiona: Beginning military reform and protecting an insecure treasury.

Golo: Investigating the awakening heartbeat within Earthshatter.

Sertraline: Learning how nature communicates and studying a device aimed toward the Ethereal Realm.

Queen Flower: Governing Veylathar while considering the much larger world beyond the city.

The Party: Positioned to choose between pursuing Jerry, repairing Veylathar's internal problems, investigating the Ethereal platform, or beginning an expedition into the wider world.

Chapter Transition

The city has survived the immediate catastrophe, but safety has revealed problems that were easier to ignore during the crisis. The garrison is neglected. Its treasury may be compromised. Earthshatter is awakening. A hidden lens continues to watch the Ethereal Realm. Beyond the throne room lies a map filled with distant cities and unknown lands.

Chapter 7 ends not with the party being given one path, but with several paths opening at once.
