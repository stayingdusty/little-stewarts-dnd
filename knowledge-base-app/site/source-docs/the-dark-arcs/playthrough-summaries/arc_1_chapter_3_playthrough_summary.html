Chapter Snapshot

After two weeks of living in Veylathar, the party stops feeling like temporary visitors. They take jobs, build goodwill, unlock new magical abilities, begin Queen Flower's political campaign, gain permanent lodging, and uncover several mysteries connected to the man in the white suit, the stolen Great Tree sap, and the Academy of Sparks.

Major Outcome

The party ends the chapter financially stable, politically active, and equipped for infiltration. Golo gains licensed Hellfire magic and flight, Sertraline awakens her druidic power, Fiona becomes a trusted healer, and Queen Flower gains a path toward securing district votes in the election for Queen Regent.

Two Weeks in Veylathar

The chapter begins after the party has spent roughly two weeks living in the city, getting its bearings, looking for work, and learning how to function within Veylathar's districts and institutions. The venison from the previous chapter has carried the group through this initial period, but Chapter 3 establishes the long-term systems that allow them to remain in the city indefinitely.

Golo Enters the Crucible District

Golo returns to the industrial Crucible District because he is fascinated by the fire rune he saw during the city tour. The district is not a consumer marketplace. It is a working forge district filled with dwarves, heavy industry, furnaces, tools, contracts, and old traditions.

  • The dwarves recognize the legendary history of partnerships between centaurs and dwarven forge-workers.
  • Golo is extraordinarily strong, making him immediately valuable, even though he begins with little technical skill.
  • He is hired as a full-day laborer for 2 gold per day.
  • The dwarven boss is a blunt, practical industrial businessman with a Boston-style accent.

The Hellfire Employment Contract

The dwarven boss offers Golo employment through a magically binding Hellfire Contract. The boss writes the terms using a special pen that traces letters in fire across parchment, sending off sparks and flame as the agreement is formed.

Important ownership rule: The dwarves already possess the Hellfire Rune through ancient bargains and agreements with other realms. Golo does not gain ownership of the rune. His employment contract licenses him limited access to its power.

  • Golo may use the Hellfire Rune three times per day or long rest.
  • Each rune use creates magical fire that lasts one hour.
  • The fire does not require fuel and does not burn out early.
  • It may be used for forge work, fire arrows, hearth fires, campfires, or other ordinary uses of flame.
  • The power only works while the Hellfire employment contract remains valid.

Milo and the Wayward Flame Hearth

Golo returns to the Wayward Flame and meets Milo, a previously unseen employee of Kara Emberfall. Milo serves as the inn's groundskeeper, stable keeper, hearth tender, and general repairman. He handles the floors, stables, mechanical upkeep, and the morning fires.

Golo offers to spend one Hellfire Rune use each morning to ignite and sustain the Wayward Flame's hearth. This saves Milo time, starter fuel, kindling, and other materials.

Permanent lodging mechanic: In exchange for one daily Hellfire use, Kara Emberfall and Milo agree that the party's lodging and meals at the Wayward Flame are covered indefinitely.

Fiona at the Healing Clinic

Fiona takes a paid job at the health clinic located inside the city market. She works under the clinic's Potions Master, a human woman whose appearance is based on the children's real-life Aunt Valerie, who is a nurse.

  • Fiona earns 2.5 gold per half-day.
  • Her healing ability functions like a cantrip, with effectively unlimited minor uses.
  • By healing patients directly, Fiona reduces the number of expensive potions the clinic must consume.
  • The position is a real job, but it also builds trust and goodwill with ordinary people throughout the city.

Sertraline Becomes a Confidential Courier

Sertraline accepts work carrying sensitive and sealed letters for the Queen Regent's guards.

  • She earns 10 gold per day.
  • The job places her close to royal correspondence, official messengers, and political intrigue.
  • Her access later allows her to recognize and interfere with the white-suited man's letters.

Sertraline's Druidic Awakening

Anja Willowbark reveals that Sertraline is filled with druidic nature magic. Anja understands this power because her own herbal cooking and recipes are infused with similar natural magic.

Anja leads Sertraline into the forest and teaches her to form a clear mental picture of what she wants from the living world around her.

  • When Sertraline wishes to be hidden, branches, trees, vines, and undergrowth help conceal her.
  • When she wishes to be revealed, the forest opens around her.
  • When she needs to pass, trees and vines bend aside to clear a path.
  • Nature may also help protect or shelter her.

Canon: Sertraline does not command nature as a machine. She influences and cooperates with the living environment through instinct, visualization, and druidic connection.

Queen Flower's First Campaign Event

Fiona and Golo decide to hold a public community event to build support for Queen Flower's challenge against the current Queen Regent. Sertraline joins the hunt, and the party gathers enough venison to operate a free venison burger stand for the public.

The event is a major success. Citizens eat for free, gather around the party, and listen to Queen Flower speak.

Queen Flower's campaign slogan:
"I will look out for the little people AND the big people!"

The crowd goes wild. By the end of the event, citizens leave talking about how wonderful Queen Flower is and spreading support for her campaign.

The Man in the White Suit Returns

At the back of the crowd stands the same mysterious man connected to the earlier cruise ship events: an older man with a white beard, an entirely white suit, and a monocle. He watches the rally and quietly walks away.

The next day, while Sertraline is working as a courier, she sees the man speaking with the Queen Regent and attempting to send several letters through official channels.

  • Sertraline projects telepathic static into his mind to interfere with his concentration.
  • She follows the messenger who accepts the letters.
  • She tracks the delivery route and destroys each letter before it can be read.
  • One letter is allowed to reach Dr. Fizzlebig.

Dr. Fizzlebig's Letter

Dr. Fizzlebig reads his letter in Sertraline's presence. It asks for information about a dignitary traveling with a pet panther and a kraken, clues that connect the inquiry to the group and the artifact taken from the cruise ship.

Fizzlebig understands that the white-suited man is trying to locate the people responsible for stealing his artifact. As a deliberate show of good faith, Fizzlebig burns the letter in front of Sertraline and promises to keep the party's secret.

  • Sertraline shows him the remaining Snap Match.
  • Fizzlebig has never seen anything like it.
  • He suspects the strange sparks and unexplained magical blips may be connected.
  • He tells the party they need to learn what the engineers are doing inside the Academy of Sparks.

The Great Tree Sap and the Feast of the Ancestors

As part of Golo's employment, the dwarves reveal that a quantity of sacred sap from the Great Tree has been stolen. The dwarves have established rights to this sap and use it during the Feast of the Ancestors.

During the feast, the sap is mixed into a great vat of ale. When the dwarves drink it together, they receive visions of earlier ancestral feasts, allowing them to see long-lost ancestors and more recently departed family members.

The Party's Dwarven Side Mission

The dwarven leadership offers the mission to the entire party, not to Golo alone.

  • 500 gold for recovering the stolen Great Tree sap, split evenly among the party.
  • 50 gold for capturing or returning the robber.
  • If the party returns either the sap or the robber, the Crucible District will cast its council vote in support of Queen Flower.
  • If the party accomplishes both objectives, both monetary rewards are paid, but the district still casts its single vote.

Election rule: Each academy or district controls one vote in the election for Queen Regent. The candidate who wins a majority of council votes becomes Queen Regent.

Enchanted Horseshoes and Golo's Flight

The party pools its money and purchases magical horseshoes from a farrier in the market. The shoes allow Golo to gallop directly into the sky.

  • Golo may fly for one hour per day or long rest.
  • He moves through the air at the same speed he normally moves on the ground.
  • The party also purchases a custom saddle system.
  • Fiona and Queen Flower may ride securely on Golo's back.
  • Sertraline rides in a side harness attached to the saddle.
  • Golo can carry the entire team while airborne.

The Dark Arcs Seen from Above

The party flies over Veylathar and gains its clearest view yet of the Dark Arcs in the sky. From this elevated position, they recognize that the arcs appear to extend outward from city centers.

This suggests that the phenomenon is not isolated to Veylathar and may connect multiple cities or population centers across the world.

Sertraline Scouts the Academy of Sparks

Following Fizzlebig's warning, Sertraline begins reconnaissance around the Academy of Sparks.

  • She sees engineers blasting or working on a globule of black goo.
  • She sneaks through a bathroom window.
  • An engineer begins to enter, stops abruptly, and leaves as though he senses that someone else is present.
  • This suggests the Academy's engineers may have unusual detection abilities or heightened suspicion.

Preparing the Infiltration

The party decides that Golo, Fiona, and Sertraline will return to infiltrate the Academy of Sparks. Their escape plan depends on using their final remaining Snap Match.

Before the mission, they purchase several types of potions from the Potions Master:

  • Long-Rest Potions: Allow the party to reset powers without waiting through a normal long rest. They purchase enough to reset their abilities twice.
  • Berserker Potions: Temporarily increase strength but leave the user needing a long rest after the effect ends.
  • Sleep Potions: Put targets to sleep.

Active Quest Threads at Chapter End

  • Infiltrate the Academy of Sparks and learn what its engineers are doing.
  • Investigate the black goo and its possible relationship to the strange sparks.
  • Recover the stolen Great Tree sap.
  • Capture the robber responsible for the theft.
  • Secure the Crucible District's vote for Queen Flower.
  • Learn more about the man in the white suit and the artifact he is trying to recover.
  • Investigate why the Dark Arcs appear to originate from city centers.

New NPCs and Expanded Roles

  • Milo: Wayward Flame groundskeeper, stable keeper, hearth tender, and repairman.
  • Dwarven Boss: Straight-talking industrial leader who employs Golo and writes Hellfire Contracts.
  • Potions Master: Human healer modeled after the children's Aunt Valerie; supervises Fiona at the market clinic.
  • Anja Willowbark: Revealed as a druidic mentor as well as the Wayward Flame's cook and bartender.
  • Dr. Fizzlebig: Becomes a trusted ally by burning the white-suited man's letter.
  • Man in the White Suit: Confirmed to be actively searching for the party connected to the cruise ship artifact theft.

Chapter Transition

Chapter 3 closes with the party no longer merely surviving in Veylathar. They now have careers, allies, political ambitions, magical resources, permanent lodging, and several overlapping mysteries. Their next move is an organized infiltration of the Academy of Sparks, where the black goo, unexplained sparks, and the engineers' secret work may reveal the first direct answers about the Dark Arcs.
