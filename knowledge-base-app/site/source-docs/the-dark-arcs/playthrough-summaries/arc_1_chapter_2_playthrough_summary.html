Chapter Snapshot

The party establishes itself in Veylathar, explores the city's magical infrastructure, uncovers the first hints of political unrest, and Fiona undergoes a life-changing transformation.

Major Outcome

The Wayward Flame becomes the party's home, Frederick becomes a friend, Queen Flower's pendant reacts to the Luminary Dome, and Fiona becomes a Paladin of Aureth.

The Wayward Flame

Kara Emberfall welcomes the party after the butcher vouches for them. Anja Willowbark serves Flameheart Ale and magical meals infused with druidic energy.

Canon: Anja unknowingly imbues her food and Flameheart Ale with druidic magic.

Kara's Map

Kara explains the city and marks the Ethereum Academy, Academy of Antiquities, Crucible District, Academy of Sparks, Council of Sparks, and Central Market with its towering blimp cargo spires.

Academy of Sparks

  • The Academy is secretly the city's magical rail hub.
  • Conductors wear enchanted boots that channel fire magic.
  • Special Sparks power trains while the match burns.
  • Employee doors are keyed to the conductor boots.
  • The party notices face-changing putty used by local children.

Frederick

The party befriends Frederick, a nervous young gnome conductor. Rather than steal from him, they encourage him. He rewards them with a free guided train tour.

Tour of Veylathar

  • Crucible District's industrial forges.
  • Council of Sparks and city government.
  • Luminary Dome where Queen Flower's pendant mysteriously rises toward the museum.
  • Ethereum Academy where Dr. Fizzlebig studies Fiona's holy transformation.

Academy of Antiquities

Queen Flower and Sertraline investigate the pendant. Outside they overhear citizens criticizing the Queen Regent, introducing the campaign's first political conflict.

Hunting Excursion

Meanwhile, Golo learns a deer call from hunters. He accidentally wounds a fawn, allowing Fiona to perform her first miraculous healing.

Downtime

The venison gathered from the hunt pays for two weeks of lodging and meals at the Wayward Flame, establishing the campaign's downtime rhythm.

Permanent Canon

  • Fiona is now a Paladin of Aureth.
  • Frederick becomes a recurring ally.
  • The Academy of Sparks and magical railway are understood.
  • Queen Flower's pendant is connected to the Luminary Dome.
  • Political dissatisfaction with the Queen Regent has begun.
  • The party has two weeks of paid room and board.
