A map for the journey

Explore the map

Drag to pan. Scroll, pinch, or use the + / − buttons to zoom. Choose a place to jump to it. 0 fits the whole map; + and − zoom. Arrow keys pan when the map has focus.

Decide what the party can see

Edit fog opens the Cover and Reveal brushes. Use Cover map to conceal everything, then trace the areas your party has explored. Mist blurs terrain; Opaque hides it completely. Peek lets the DM see underneath while editing. Closing the editor ends the peek.

The Fog switch temporarily shows or hides your existing fog. The Encounters switch shows or hides the twelve numbers. Covered encounter markers stay hidden while fog is on. E toggles encounters; F toggles fog; Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes a fog edit.

Keep everything in one file

The map works offline. Your browser remembers edits on this device. Save map downloads an HTML copy containing your current fog and encounter settings. Open it directly or commit it to your repository. Publishing a new saved copy gives it a new storage revision, so old device edits do not override the new defaults.
